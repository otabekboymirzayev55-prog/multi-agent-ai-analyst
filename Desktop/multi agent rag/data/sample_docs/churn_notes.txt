Q2 2025 Customer Churn Notes — Internal Summary

Blue Harbor Inc (Starter plan) churned in April 2025. Exit interview notes indicate
the primary reason was price sensitivity — the customer said a competitor offered a
similar tool at a lower monthly cost, and Blue Harbor's usage had been declining for
two months before cancellation.

Crestline Foods (Pro plan) churned in May 2025. Their support tickets show repeated
complaints about a missing integration with their inventory system. The sales team
had promised a workaround that was never delivered, which was the stated reason for
leaving in their cancellation survey.

Ember Studio (Starter plan) churned in April 2025 due to low product usage. Login
logs show the account was nearly inactive for the six weeks before cancellation.
The customer did not respond to two re-engagement emails.

Harborlight Co (Starter plan) churned in May 2025. Their stated reason was budget
cuts following a change in company leadership, unrelated to product satisfaction.

Junction Media (Starter plan) churned in May 2025. Support notes mention frequent
complaints about slow page-load times in the reporting dashboard, which the
engineering team confirmed was a real performance issue during that period.

General pattern: most churn this quarter came from the Starter plan, and the two
most common reasons were (1) price sensitivity relative to competitors and
(2) low engagement/usage before cancellation. Only one case (Crestline) involved a
missing feature, and only one case (Harborlight) was unrelated to the product.
