Product Overview — Internal Reference

Our product has three pricing tiers: Starter ($99/month), Pro ($499/month), and
Enterprise ($1,499/month). Starter customers get core reporting features only.
Pro customers get the reporting dashboard plus the inventory integration and
priority support. Enterprise customers get everything in Pro plus a dedicated
account manager and custom SLAs.

The reporting dashboard is the most-used feature across all tiers. In Q1 2025 we
shipped a performance upgrade to the dashboard, but a regression in Q2 2025
temporarily slowed page-load times for some Starter accounts before it was fixed
in June 2025.

The inventory integration was requested most often by Pro-tier retail and food
customers. It became generally available in July 2025, after several customers
had already left without it.

Customer success runs a re-engagement email sequence for any account with no
login activity for 30 days, followed by a second email at 45 days if there is
still no activity.
